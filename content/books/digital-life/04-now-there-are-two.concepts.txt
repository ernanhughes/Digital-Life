WHAT YOU NEED TO KNOW

PERSISTENCE VS REPRODUCTION

Persistence means one organization continues. Reproduction means an earlier organization causes later organizations of the same kind to appear.

CAUSAL DESCENT

A later structure depends on an earlier one for its existence. Looking similar is not enough.

```
similarity
does not equal
ancestry
```

BRANCHING HISTORY

For reproduction, one earlier organization must give rise to at least two later ones. A single chain of repeated appearances is not enough.

OUTLIER

A binary cellular automaton whose local rule was selected by search, not hand-written as a reproduction function. Replicating structures were discovered inside the rule's world.

MOORE NEIGHBOURHOOD

The 3 by 3 block around a cell, including the cell itself. Outlier's rule reads that local pattern to decide the next cell state.

CAUSAL ANCESTRY GRAPH

A graph linking earlier clusters to later clusters only when measured cell-level dependencies connect them.

RECURRENCE, REPLICATION, SUSTAINED LINEAGE

Recurrence means a structure appears again. Replication means a copy causally depends on an earlier structure. A sustained lineage means descendants continue reproducing across generations.

DISTRIBUTED REPLICATION

Replication that involves separated components rather than one connected body. The reproducing process need not be a neat object.

PRE-SPECIFIED TARGET

A structure chosen before searching the data. This prevents choosing whichever shape happens to recur most impressively afterward.
