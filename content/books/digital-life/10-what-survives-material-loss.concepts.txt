WHAT YOU NEED TO KNOW

MATERIAL LOSS

Occupied cells can become empty again. This removes the earlier guarantee that occupied material stays forever.

FINITE SUSTAINABLE SIZE

A predicted balance where construction and loss stop net growth at a stable size. The tested loss sweep did not establish this.

GROSS CONSTRUCTION

Total attachment events, regardless of how many cells are lost. Gross construction can rise even when net population changes only gently.

CONSTRUCTION INTERFACE

The set of empty locations currently eligible for attachment. With loss, this can appear inside the crystal, not only at the outer edge.

REOCCUPATION

A location that was occupied, became empty, and becomes occupied again.

```
FIRST OCCUPATION
new site used for the first time

REOCCUPATION
old site returns after loss
```

OBSERVER-ONLY LEDGER

A record kept by the experimenter, not read by the crystal. It lets the laboratory distinguish first occupation from reoccupation.

REPAIR

A special mechanism that detects damage or restores a target state. Rapid reoccupation is not repair because the crystal has no such mechanism.

STOCK VS FLOW

Population is a stock: how much is occupied now. Attachments, losses, and reoccupations are flows: how much material activity is happening.

STRUCTURAL COMPENSATION

Loss creates new attachment opportunities, and ordinary construction reuses many of them. This counter-effect does not imply intention or maintenance.
