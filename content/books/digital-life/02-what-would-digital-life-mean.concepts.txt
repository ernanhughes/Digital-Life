WHAT YOU NEED TO KNOW

CARGO CULT

Copying the outward look of something without reproducing the mechanism that makes it work. In this chapter, naming code after life-like properties is treated as cargo-cult evidence.

BIOLOGY AS EVIDENCE, NOT SPECIFICATION

Biology proves that life is possible in one medium. It does not prove that digital life must use the same parts or solve the same problems the same way.

OPERATIONAL DEFINITION

A definition stated in terms of what would be observed or measured. Before calling something memory or reproduction, the chapter asks what result would count for or against that claim.

INTERVENTION

Deliberately changing or removing something to see what difference it makes. If removing a proposed mechanism does not change the result, the explanation was probably wrong.

CONTROL

A comparison case designed to rule out a simpler explanation. A control asks, "Would this have happened anyway?"

CONFOUND

Something mixed into the result that can produce the same measurement for a different reason. Finding a confound means the old interpretation was carrying more than one possible explanation.

BOUNDED CLAIM

A claim whose conditions and limits are visible. Instead of "the system heals," the book prefers claims that say what was changed, what was measured, and what comparison survived.

PHENOMENON VS EXPLANATION

The phenomenon is what happened. The explanation is the story about why it happened.

```
beautiful animation
does not equal
correct interpretation
```

SUBSTRATE

The medium in which the process runs. Biology uses physical matter; these experiments use computation, which has different costs and possibilities.
