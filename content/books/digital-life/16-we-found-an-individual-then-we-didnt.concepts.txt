WHAT YOU NEED TO KNOW

CAUSAL CONTAINMENT

Perturbations started inside a region express more of their causal effect inside than outside. This can be measured without proving individuality.

INTERNAL RETENTION

The fraction of causal mass from an internal perturbation that stays inside the region.

EXTERNAL PENETRATION

The fraction of causal mass from an external perturbation that appears inside the region.

MODULARITY SCORE

Internal retention minus external penetration. A high score means strong raw containment.

ABSOLUTE CAUSAL MASS

The accumulated size of probability shifts, ignoring sign. It measures where influence is expressed, not whether positive and negative shifts cancel.

SPATIAL NULL

A comparison region with similar geometry. It asks whether the observed score is more than what ordinary spatial layout already produces.

RAW MODULARITY VS EXCESS MODULARITY

Raw modularity measures containment. Excess modularity asks whether selected regions beat matched controls.

OBSERVER-DEFINED BOUNDARY

A boundary imposed by the measurement. It creates inside and outside categories even if the system does not privilege them.

SYSTEM-PRIVILEGED BOUNDARY

A boundary the dynamics distinguish beyond matched geometry. This was not established.

CAUSAL CONTAINMENT VS INDIVIDUATION

Containment is real here. Individuation would require privilege beyond comparable spatial geometry.
