WHAT YOU NEED TO KNOW

ESTIMAND

The specific quantity an experiment is built to estimate. Example: excess modularity over matched spatial controls.

CONSTRUCT

The richer idea the experiment hopes to support. Example: individuality, memory, repair, or flocking.

SMALLEST EFFECT OF INTEREST (SEI)

The predeclared effect size large enough to matter scientifically for that estimand.

MINIMUM DETECTABLE EFFECT AT 80% POWER (MDE80)

How small an effect the experiment had enough precision to detect under its frozen analysis convention.

INVALID

The experiment did not implement the intended contrast. It cannot count as evidence for or against that intended claim.

UNRESOLVED

The experiment was valid but not precise enough to answer the declared question at the required scale.

BOUNDED

A meaningful effect region was excluded with enough precision. "Bounded near zero" and "bounded below SEI" are different kinds of bounded result.

NARROWED

A richer interpretation fails while a smaller measurement remains valid.

```
raw containment survives
v
individuality does not
```

DESCRIPTIVE ONLY

An analysis that explains or contextualizes a result but does not change the frozen confirmatory status.

FAIL THE SMALLEST CLAIM

When a claim fails, remove only the part the evidence actually defeats. Keep the surviving measurement, mechanism, or narrower phenomenon.
