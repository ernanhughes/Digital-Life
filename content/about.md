## Active Projects

### ✍️ Writer

A writing too to enhance your output using AI.

### 🕵️ Stephanie
Stephanie is a local assistant that constantly indexes your ideas, files, and context, bringing the right information into your current conversation automatically. It is like having a memory-augmented subconscious AI.

[GitHub: Stephanie](https://github.com/ernanhughes/stephanie)

### 📺 Too Much YouTube
This tool filters your YouTube subscriptions and transcripts to find only what is truly relevant, using your current session context to highlight key segments worth watching.

[GitHub: Too Much YouTube](https://github.com/ernanhughes/tmy)

### 🧬 Cognitive Assistant (Co)
This is an evolving experiment in building a true AI collaborator, blending prompt templates, memory, reasoning, and rhythm. It ties into the theories behind Freestyle Cognition and powers many of the book’s sessions.

[GitHub: Co](https://github.com/ernanhughes/co)

## Research

My recent paper, *Cross-Modal Cognitive Mapping*, explores a method for modeling human thought by linking language, visual generation, and user selection behavior. The framework aims to better understand how people conceptualize ideas across cultures and individuals, with potential applications in AI alignment, cognitive science, and personalized systems.
