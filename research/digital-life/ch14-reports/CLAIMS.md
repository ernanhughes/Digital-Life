# Chapter 14 Claims

## Supported

A fixed local growth rule transforms differences in
forcing-source families into morphology from which source
class can be recovered above chance.

Evidence:
- 600 crystal primary experiment
- RF 0.522 vs 0.167 chance
- logistic 0.539 vs 0.167 chance
- forcing robustness sweep

## Not supported

Final morphology preserves temporal ordering.

Evidence:
- ordered/shuffled: ~0.51 vs 0.50 chance
- matched-distribution temporal experiment: no above-chance recovery

- life
- memory
